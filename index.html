Measure a home in one walkthrough.

LiDAR Home Scanner uses the LiDAR scanner in iPhone Pro and iPad Pro to turn a walk through a home into a measured floor plan, with room areas and ceiling heights, a 3D model and a 360° photo walkthrough. Object mode turns small things into 3D models you can print.

  • Floor plans with interior areas and ceiling heights, labelled as estimates
  • 3D view of every floor
  • 360° room photos and a walkthrough you can share by AirDrop or Messages
  • Object scans exported as USDZ, OBJ and STL, in millimetres for 3D printing
  • Room check, to compare the plan with your own laser readings

Everything is worked out on your device, and your scans stay there.
