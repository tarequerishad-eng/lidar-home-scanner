Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email tareque.rishad@icloud.com.

Reporting a problem? Shake your phone right after it happens to flag the moment, then go to Settings › Diagnostics › Share diagnostics and email us the log. Please say which device you used.

What you need

  • Scanning rooms: an iPhone Pro or iPad Pro with a LiDAR scanner (iPhone 12 Pro or later Pro model, or iPad Pro 2020 or later), running iOS 17 or later.
  • Scanning objects: an iPhone Pro or iPad Pro with LiDAR and an A14 chip or later.
  • Other iPhones and iPads can open the built-in sample house and walkthrough files that other people share with them.

Tips for a good scan

  • Turn on the lights and open doors between rooms.
  • Walk slowly and point the phone at every wall, from the floor up to the ceiling.
  • When the app says "Start next room", hold the phone still for a second before you move on.
  • Scan one floor at a time. Add other floors later with Scan more rooms.

Common questions

How accurate are the measurements?
They are estimates. Areas are interior areas, measured to the inside face of the walls. Check important numbers with a laser measure; Room check lets you compare them in the app.

Where are my scans stored?
Only on your device. The app has no account and sends nothing over the internet. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I share a floor plan or tour?
Open the scan and use the share buttons to send a PDF, images, a 3D model or a walkthrough file by AirDrop, Messages, Mail or Files.

How do I delete a scan?
Swipe left on it, or open it and use Delete in Details. Deleted scans stay in the app's trash for 7 days.

Feet or metres?
Choose in Settings › Units.
