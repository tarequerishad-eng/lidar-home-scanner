Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026 · applies to LiDAR Home Scanner for iPhone and iPad, version 1.0

In short: LiDAR Home Scanner does not collect any personal data. Everything you scan stays on your device. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it doesn't send your data over the internet.

Who we are

LiDAR Home Scanner is made by Tareque Rahman Rishad ("we", "us"). You can reach us at tareque.rishad@icloud.com.

What stays on your device

The app uses your device's camera and LiDAR scanner, with your permission, to measure rooms and objects. Everything it makes and everything you type is stored only in the app's own storage on your device:

  • room and object scans, floor plans, ceiling heights and 3D models;
  • photos taken during a scan, such as 360° room photos and object photos;
  • names and addresses you type for properties and rooms, and any readings you enter in Room check.

All the measuring and model building happens on your device. We never receive any of it.

Diagnostics

To help fix problems, the app keeps a technical log on your device: what the app was doing, errors, your device model and iOS version. It does not contain your photos or 3D models. The log is never sent anywhere automatically. It leaves your device only if you choose Settings › Diagnostics › Share diagnostics and pick where to send it. You can delete it at any time with Clear logs.

Sharing

When you export a floor plan, image, 3D model or walkthrough file, the standard iOS share sheet opens and you choose where it goes, for example AirDrop, Messages, Mail, Files or your photo library. We don't see what you share. Walkthrough files that other people send you are opened and kept on your device.

Permissions

  • Camera: to scan rooms and objects. Camera images are processed on your device.
  • Add to Photos: only when you choose to save an image to your photo library from the share sheet.

You can change these permissions at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Backups

Depending on your device settings, iOS may include the app's data in your iCloud or computer backups. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Deleting your data

Delete a scan or object in the app. Deleted scans are kept in the app's trash for 7 days and then removed. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

A future version may add optional online features, such as an account or web links to share a home tour. Before any of your data leaves your device, we will update this policy and the app's privacy details on the App Store, and those features will be optional. The effective date above always shows the latest version.
